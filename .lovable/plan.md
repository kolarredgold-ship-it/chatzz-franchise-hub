

# Bangarpet ChatzZ – Franchise & Training Landing Page

## Overview
A vibrant, mobile-first single-page landing website for Bangarpet ChatzZ to attract franchise partners and trainees. The design will use an **orange & red street food color palette** with warm tones to evoke the energy of Indian street food culture.

**Phone & WhatsApp:** 9738959525

---

## Sections to Build

### 1. Hero Section
- Bold brand name "Bangarpet ChatzZ" with tagline: *"Authentic Bangarpet-Style Chaats – Now Across India"*
- Short 2-line description about the franchise opportunity
- Two prominent CTA buttons: **Call Now** (click-to-call) and **WhatsApp Franchise Enquiry** (opens WhatsApp chat with pre-filled message)
- Warm, appetizing background styling

### 2. About the Brand
- Short storytelling section (~120 words) about the origin of Bangarpet chaat
- Focus on taste consistency, hygiene, and authenticity
- Clean, readable layout with a decorative accent

### 3. Menu Showcase
- Organized into categories: **Pani Puri Variants**, **Puri & Masala Items**, **Bhel Varieties**, **Special & Fusion Items**, **Drinks**
- "Best Seller" badges on: Masala Puri, Sev Puri, Spicy Dahi Boondi, Sprouts Bhel
- Text-based card layout initially — images can be added later when the menu PDF is uploaded
- Pricing disclaimer at the bottom of the section

### 4. Business Models
- Two clearly separated cards/sections:
  - **Training Model** – ₹50,000, 5–7 days, details of what's included
  - **Brand & Business Model** – ₹1,50,000, full brand rights, no royalty, no monthly fees
- Visual comparison to help visitors decide

### 5. Why Choose Bangarpet ChatzZ
- Icon-driven bullet points: Authentic taste, no royalty, simple operations, high-demand menu, small space friendly, strong local appeal

### 6. Franchise Partner Expectations
- Brief section outlining: maintain standards, follow SOPs, no recipe/brand misuse

### 7. Enquiry Form (Lead Capture)
- Fields: Name, Mobile Number (required), City/Area, Interested Model (dropdown)
- On submit → redirects to WhatsApp with the form data pre-filled as a message
- Additional CTAs: Click to Call, WhatsApp Chat buttons

### 8. Footer
- Phone number, website URL, social links placeholders
- Copyright notice

---

## Design & UX
- **Mobile-first** responsive design
- **Orange & Red** warm color palette with food-inspired accents
- Clean, easy-to-read fonts
- Smooth scroll navigation between sections
- Sticky header with navigation links
- SEO meta tags and semantic HTML structure

---

## Technical Notes
- No backend required — form submissions go to WhatsApp
- Click-to-call uses `tel:` links, WhatsApp uses the WhatsApp API URL
- All content is static, no database needed
- Menu images can be added later when the PDF is uploaded
