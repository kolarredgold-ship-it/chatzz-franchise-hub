# Chatzz Franchise Hub

# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Name

**Bangarpet ChatzZ – Franchise & Training Webpage**

---

## 1. Objective

The objective of this webpage is to:

* Attract potential franchise partners and trainees
* Clearly explain Bangarpet ChatzZ business models
* Generate qualified leads via calls, WhatsApp, and enquiry forms
* Build brand trust and authenticity

Primary CTA: **Franchise Enquiry / Call / WhatsApp**

---

## 2. Target Audience

* Street food entrepreneurs
* First-time food business owners
* Small restaurant / fast food operators
* Individuals seeking Bangarpet chaat making training
* Investors in Tier-2 & Tier-3 cities

---

## 3. Page Type

* Single scrolling landing page
* Mobile-first design
* Fast loading (SEO optimized)

### 4.4 Business Models Section

**Purpose:** Explain offerings clearly

#### Model 1: Bangarpet Chaat Making Training

* One-time training program
* Fee: ₹50,000
* Duration: 5–7 days
* Includes:

  * Complete chaat making training
  * Hygiene & preparation SOPs
  * Ingredient sourcing guidance
  * Training completion certificate
* Brand usage: Not allowed

---

#### Model 2: Bangarpet ChatzZ Brand & Business Model

* One-time brand association
* Fee: ₹1,50,000
* Includes:

  * Brand name usage rights
  * Complete training
  * Raw material support (initial phase)
  * Secret masala & formulations
  * SOPs & menu guidance
  * Branding & launch support
* Royalty: Nil
* Monthly fees: Nil
